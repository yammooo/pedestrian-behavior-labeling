## Contribution boundaries and review gaps

| Topic | Existing evidence / boundary | Current research implication | Review still required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Automatic crossing pseudo-labeling | [Riaz et al. 2025](minimizing-human-labeling.md) already reports binary self-labeling and smoothing | Automatic labels/smoothing alone are not a contribution | Target access, label budgets, temporal semantics and independent audits |
| Native actions versus future intention | [LOKI](../datasets/loki.md) shifts actions for prediction; neighboring work includes recognition and framewise segmentation as well as prediction | RQ1 reconstructs current states; the offline formulation alone is insufficient novelty | Annotation definitions, ROAD-Waymo projection and full protocol comparison with recognition work |
| Recoverability and modality contribution | [MulCPred](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09446) studies multimodal concepts; local LOKI examples show weak evidence | RQ1 compares evidence, classes and observation conditions; poor classification is not sensor-insufficiency proof | Comparable modality/temporal controls, human-evidence audit and limits of identifiability claims |
| Heterogeneous partial supervision | [Zhou & Zeng](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0921889023002191) uses partial pedestrian annotations across datasets | RQ2 compares complementary supervision against single-dataset end-task training | Closest temporal behavior methods, missing-modality objectives and matched data/compute/access controls |
| Non-identical taxonomies | [MSeg](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Lambert_MSeg_A_Composite_Dataset_for_Multi-Domain_Semantic_Segmentation_CVPR_2020_paper.html) establishes risks of naive merging in segmentation | Preserve semantics; lightweight native heads are a candidate | Precise audit and shared/native-head comparisons without assumed equivalence |
| Scene grounding and factorization | Scene/road relation may distinguish Crossing/Waiting beyond motion | Methods under RQ1/RQ2, not separate RQs or settled factors | Road-relative/3D scene baselines, factor identifiability and simpler shared representations |
| Cross-dataset and visibility selection | [Gesnouin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12626) and [MulCPred](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09446) establish prediction-transfer failures | ROAD-Waymo↔LOKI is useful but geography/sensors/semantics/population confound asymmetry | Population-aware evaluation and closest RGB-to-3D transfer/distillation work |
| Label efficiency, negative transfer and selective labeling | More supervision can conflict; reliable coverage needs independent evidence | Equal target-label access, retained negative ablations, uncertainty only after error analysis | Budget/compute accounting, learning-curve variability, calibration and independent audit |

Our proposed synthesis is a general offline track-based behavior labeler with 2D/3D multimodal evidence, explicit recoverability analysis and heterogeneous supervision across differing modalities/taxonomies. This is an interpretation of the handoff and neighboring evidence, not an established novelty claim. Individual ingredients already exist; results and a closer task/protocol comparison must establish any distinctive contribution. E001 is an initial diagnostic, not the final method or contribution.

[ARCANE-PedSynth](../datasets/pedsynth-plusplus.md) remains reported rich-synthetic motivation with limited inspected usability. Its former label-efficiency hypothesis is preserved in the [synthetic investigation](../archive/2026-10-01-synthetic-supervision.md); revisit only with new suitable supervision. Missing reviews are not evidence of novelty.
