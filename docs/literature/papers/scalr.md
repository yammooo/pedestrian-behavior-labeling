# Three Pillars improving Vision Foundation Model Distillation for Lidar

Puy et al., CVPR 2024; ScaLR. Initial candidate screen **2026-10-15**: [primary paper record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17504), [official implementation/checkpoint table](https://github.com/valeoai/ScaLR) and [ScaLR+ branch](https://github.com/valeoai/ScaLR/tree/scalr_plus). This is an interface/provenance screen, not a full paper review or reproduction.

ScaLR distills image features into a WaffleIron LiDAR backbone. The listed unannotated-pretraining checkpoint uses DINOv2 ViT-L/14 supervision from paired nuScenes, SemanticKITTI and PandaSet data (Pandar64 and PandarGT sensors). The main table links a WI-48-768 backbone plus distillation head; separate linear-probe and fully fine-tuned checkpoints have additional semantic-label exposure. ScaLR+ changes the training recipe and publishes a separate pretrained backbone; it is identified as an improved version used in IGLOSS.

**Important distinction:** a pretrained point branch can use LiDAR at inference while carrying RGB-derived knowledge from training. Its spatial features could supply scene semantics without coloring our clouds or requiring camera calibration at deployment. This does not recover image details absent from the current point cloud and does not make a dense RGB branch redundant. Describe experiments as LiDAR-only inference with image-supervised pretraining, rather than exclusively LiDAR-trained representations.

The listed point-training corpora do not include Waymo/LOKI. DINOv2's web-data intersection remains unknown; the exact backbone, teacher, release and any downstream tuning still need audit. For future nuScenes auxiliary supervision, all arms must share initialization/exposure. Required point features, especially intensity, normalization, coordinate scale and behavior on sparse LOKI clouds remain unverified. No weight was downloaded or executed.

**Relevance:** a promising pretrained outdoor alternative to [Utonia](utonia.md) when deliberate Waymo point-pretraining is undesirable. Contrast with [BEVContrast](bevcontrast.md), whose self-supervision does not require an image teacher. Neither model is selected by this screen. [Direction review](../offline-labeling-research-direction.md).

## Performance comparison limits (2026-10-16)

[Paper Tables 4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.17504v2) report nuScenes validation mIoU **67.8** with a frozen multi-dataset WI-768 encoder and linear probing, and **78.4** after full segmentation fine-tuning. The no-pretraining WI-768 baseline reaches 78.7 with all nuScenes labels; ScaLR's benefits are more apparent under limited labels, domain transfer and corruption than an automatic improvement in fully supervised in-domain segmentation. The same paper reports multi-dataset versus nuScenes-only initialization improving transfer to unseen SemanticPOSS from 57.1 to 59.1 mIoU after nuScenes fine-tuning (Table 7), using aggregated classes; this is useful supporting evidence, not a pedestrian-state result.

[D-DITR](ditr.md#performance-and-rgb-branch-interpretation-2026-10-16) reports 80.9 nuScenes mIoU after fine-tuning PTv3. Different backbones, exposure and recipes prevent treating that as a controlled head-to-head distillation comparison. WaffleIron is not PTv3; both methods can distill DINOv2 while retaining different 3D architectures. A DINO RGB branch remains an independent current-observation path; image-derived pretraining does not make it redundant. Frozen-feature behavior transfer and inference cost on our clouds remain unmeasured.
